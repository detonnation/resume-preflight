How it works

It pulls out the job’s requirements, then shows exactly what your résumé supports.

  1. Paste the job description

    It pulls out the actual requirements and sets aside the filler.

  2. Add your résumé

    Each requirement gets matched to the specific lines that support it, or flagged when nothing does.

  3. Confirm what counts

    When it finds related evidence, it suggests a match. You decide. Nothing counts until you confirm it.

What it won't do

Most résumé tools are built to make you feel better. This one is built to make the gap visible.

  • Invent experience you don't haveIf your résumé doesn't say it, it won't pretend it does.
  • Tell you to stuff in keywordsMatching words isn't the same as having the experience. It looks for evidence.
  • Hide its reasoningEvery result points back to the line it came from, so you can check it.
  • Score your personality"Team player" and "detail-oriented" get set aside for the interview, where they belong.
