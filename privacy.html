Privacy

What the waitlist collects

Right now this site does one thing: it collects email addresses for the Resume Preflight waitlist. Here's exactly what that involves.

What's collected

When you join the waitlist, the site saves:

  • the email address you enter
  • the date and time you signed up
  • which form you used (the one at the top of the page or the one at the bottom)
  • the referrer: the web address your browser reports for the page the form was submitted from

That's it. No résumé or job description is collected on this site.

Where it's stored

Signups are stored privately in this site's own storage on Vercel, the company that hosts the site. They aren't publicly accessible. If you sign up twice with the same address, it's saved once.

How it's used

Your address is used only to contact you about Resume Preflight: the waitlist and its launch.

What doesn't happen

Your email address is not sold, and it's not shared with anyone for advertising.

Services this page relies on

Like any website, the site is served by its host (Vercel), and fonts load from Google Fonts. Those services receive standard technical information, such as your IP address, when your browser requests the page.

Questions or removal

If you want your address removed from the waitlist or have a question, message Resume Preflight on LinkedIn.
